What is Google Merchant Center, and does your store need it?

How your products get onto Google Shopping, why they get disapproved, and whether it's worth setting up for your store.

When someone searches Google for a product, they often see a row of product images with prices, before any normal website links. Those come from Google Shopping, and the way your products get there is Google Merchant Center.

If you sell physical products online and you're not on Google Shopping, you're missing people who are already searching for what you sell.

What Google Merchant Center does

Merchant Center is a free Google tool where you upload information about your products: titles, prices, images, stock and shipping. Google then uses that information to show your products:

  • In free listings on Google Shopping and other Google surfaces, at no cost
  • In Shopping ads, if you choose to run paid campaigns through Google Ads

Many store owners don't realise the free listings exist. You can start there before spending anything on ads.

Does your store need it?

It's worth setting up if:

  • You sell physical products people search for by name or type
  • Your prices are competitive, because shoppers compare side by side
  • You want sales from people who are already looking to buy, not only from social media

It's less useful if you sell services, custom work that can't be priced in advance, or products that Google's policies don't allow.

Why products get disapproved

Setting up the account is the easy part. Keeping products approved is where most stores struggle. Common reasons I see:

  • The price or availability in the feed doesn't match the website
  • Missing details, such as product identifiers, brand or shipping information
  • The website is missing clear contact details, return policy or secure checkout
  • Product images with text, watermarks or borders over them
  • Products or claims that break Google's Shopping policies

What a proper setup includes

  • Merchant Center account created and your website verified
  • Shipping and return settings that match your store
  • A product feed connected to your store, so changes update automatically
  • Disapproved products checked and fixed one by one
  • Free listings switched on, and Shopping ads ready when you want them
