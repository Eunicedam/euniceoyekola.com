Meta Ads for local businesses: how to get WhatsApp messages and bookings

How restaurants, salons, clinics and other local businesses can use Facebook and Instagram ads to fill their WhatsApp and their calendar.

Meta Ads aren't only for online stores. If you run a restaurant, salon, clinic, gym, school or real estate business, Facebook and Instagram can put you in front of the people who live and work near you, and bring them straight into your WhatsApp.

Here's how I set up campaigns for local businesses, step by step.

1. Pick one clear goal

A local campaign works best when it asks for one action. Decide which one matters most right now:

  • WhatsApp messages: best for salons, restaurants, shops and services where people like to ask questions first.
  • Lead forms: best for real estate, schools, clinics and anything where you need a name and phone number to follow up.
  • Bookings or calls: best when you have a booking link or someone ready to answer the phone.

2. Target the area you actually serve

Set your location around your business and choose a radius people would realistically travel. A salon might use a few kilometres. A real estate agency or a school might go wider. Showing ads to people who will never visit is the fastest way to waste a local budget.

3. Make ads that feel local

People respond to real places and real faces. Polished stock photos often do worse than a short, honest video.

  • Show your space, your team and your work: the plate of food, the finished hairstyle, the classroom.
  • Mention the area by name, so people know you're near them.
  • Give a simple reason to message today, such as a first-visit offer or limited slots this week.

4. Be ready when they message

This is where many local campaigns fail. Someone sends a WhatsApp message, and nobody replies for hours. By then they've booked somewhere else.

  • Set up a WhatsApp Business greeting message and quick replies for common questions.
  • Decide who answers messages and how fast. Within minutes is ideal.
  • Have your prices, location and opening hours ready to send.

5. Track what the ads really bring in

Count messages, leads and bookings, but also ask new customers how they found you. A campaign that brings 50 messages but no customers needs a different offer or a better follow-up, not more budget.

A simple starting plan

  • One goal, one area, one clear offer
  • Two or three short videos to test against each other
  • A fixed testing budget for the first two weeks
  • Fast replies on WhatsApp, every day
